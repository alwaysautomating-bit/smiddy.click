QUESTIONS

Asked and answered.

How much does AI consulting cost with Blue Dot?

The prices are published: the AI Opportunity Session is $995, fixed. Workflow Sprints are $4,500 (about two weeks) or $8,500 (three to four weeks), fixed. The Fractional AI Implementation Lead is $5,000 a month. The AI Use Policy Pack is $1,500, fixed.

Why publish prices?

Because you should know what something costs before you spend an hour on a call about it. Scope and timing are stated up front too, and you can stop after any step.

Is this a chatbot or a dashboard?

No. AI should do the work: not create another interface your employees have to manage, not generate another document someone has to process by hand, and not require everyone to become a prompt engineer.

Do we have to buy new software?

Usually not. Your existing systems often do more than anyone has had time to set up. We start with what you already pay for. AI tool fees, when needed, typically run $50 to $500 a month for a small team and are paid directly to the vendors.

What if AI isn't the answer?

Then we'll say so. If plain automation or a better checklist solves it, that's what we'll recommend. Some work is best left exactly as it is.
